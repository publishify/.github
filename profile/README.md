### A writing editor made for books, with an editorial board beside it

**Write.** Publishify is a writing editor that runs in your browser. Plan your book as parts › chapters › scenes, write in a quiet editor where Markdown formats itself, and keep your work in your browser or your account. No installation, no account needed to start.

**Refine.** When you want other eyes on it, the **Publishify editorial board** reads your book inside your own AI application. An Editor, a Continuity Checker, a panel of three readers and an Editor-in-Chief give notes that each rest on a quotation from your text, and a dashboard with the Wayfarer's Seven Stages map. Your book never goes to Publishify's servers.

**Publish.** Export your book as Word, EPUB, PDF or Markdown for a publisher, a store or your own channel.

### Türkçe
